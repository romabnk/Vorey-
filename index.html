The problem

People don't need more options. They need the answer.

Every app hands locals and travelers an endless list to scroll. Vorey gives one decision: the right place, right now, based on who you are and what's happening around you.

The market

A huge market, starting in one of its richest corners.

The Costa del Sol draws more than 14 million visitors and over 21 billion euros in tourism spend every year. Marbella sits at the luxury heart of it, dense with restaurants, beach clubs and the residents and expats who live here all year round.

The model

Free for users. Paid by merchants, only for results.

Vorey is free for everyone who uses it. Merchants pay around 1.50 euros for each verified real visit, confirmed by location. No clicks, no impressions, no wasted spend. Revenue grows directly with the real customers we deliver.
