VOREY

Privacy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Vorey is a local recommendation app for Marbella, opening on 28 January 2027. Before launch, you can join the list, get your Founding Card and follow the Vorey Drops. Here is exactly what we do with your data, in plain words.

Who is responsible

Kebane Cecylia Lymar, founder of Vorey (company being incorporated), Marbella, Spain. Contact: contact@vorey.app.

What we collect

  • Your first name and email address, and the language you chose.
  • Your Founding number, who invited you (if anyone) and the friends who joined with your link.
  • The date you gave your consent, and where you came from (for example a QR code or Instagram).
  • In your Vorey space: the Drops you open, the posts you see and your reactions.

Why

  • To keep your place and your Founding Card, and let you open your Vorey space.
  • To send you Victor’s Drops every two weeks, and tell you when Vorey opens.
  • To run the draws, tell winners and give them their prize.
  • To count your chances (each friend who confirms their email = +1).

We never sell or share your data, and we never show it to venues. Venues never pay to be chosen.

Legal basis

Your consent, given when you join. You can withdraw it at any time: one click at the bottom of every Drop email stops them, and a message to contact@vorey.app removes you from the list.

Where your data lives

In the European Union: our database is hosted by Supabase in Paris (France), and our emails are sent by Brevo (France).

How long

Until 12 months after the launch of the Vorey app, unless you create a Vorey account or ask us to erase your data sooner.

Your rights

You can access, correct, erase or export your data, or object to its use, by writing to contact@vorey.app. You can also complain to the Spanish data protection authority (AEPD, aepd.es).
